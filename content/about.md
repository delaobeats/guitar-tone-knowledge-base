# About Me

My name is Gabriel De La O, and I'm a guitarist, music producer, and Computing Applications student at Texas Tech University.

Music has been a major part of my life for many years. I've performed in bands, experimented with guitar pedals and amplifiers, and explored music production using software such as Ableton Live.

I created this knowledge base to document what I've learned about guitar tone, effects pedals, and sound design. I'm particularly interested in how analog and digital technology can work together to help musicians create their own sonic identity.

Through this project, I hope to combine my passion for music with the technical skills I'm developing through my education.
